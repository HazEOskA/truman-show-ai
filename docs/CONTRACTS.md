# Subsystem contracts

Generated by `python scripts/dump_contracts.py` from the system registry — this is
what the kernel enforces, not a description of it. With `strict_contracts` enabled a
system that writes a domain missing from its `writes` column is recorded as a failure
and quarantined.

Phases run in a fixed order within every tick:
`ENVIRONMENT → AGENTS → INSTITUTIONS → MARKETS → PHYSICAL → INFORMATION → SLOW`.

## Descriptions

- **`weather`** — Seasonal temperature, precipitation and wind; drives heating and cooling load.
- **`power_grid`** — Merit-order dispatch, reliability per district, electricity supply to market.
- **`water`** — Water production, constrained by power reliability, published to the market.
- **`transport`** — Commuting load per district; congestion feeds logistics cost and mood.
- **`dormancy`** — ACTIVE/LIGHT_IDLE/SLEEP/DORMANT/OFFSCREEN lifecycle, sleep skip and wake deltas.
- **`perception`** — Builds per-agent observations from local, observable conditions only.
- **`agent_brains`** — Utility-AI decisions with optional, budgeted LLM escalation for Tier A.
- **`government`** — Daily policy decisions from economy, unrest, approval and fiscal position.
- **`company_decisions`** — Margin, cash and demand drive pricing, output, hiring, layoffs, bankruptcy.
- **`payroll`** — Pays wages to individuals and cohort staff; collects income tax.
- **`housing`** — Dwelling stock, monthly rent, arrears and eviction pressure.
- **`taxes`** — Monthly corporate tax, budget balance and public debt.
- **`markets`** — Price formation from supply, demand, inventory, cost and expectations.
- **`market_close`** — Ends the market cycle: clears per-cycle supply, demand and turnover.
- **`labour_market`** — Job matching for individuals and cohorts; unemployment and wage index.
- **`banking`** — Services loans, records defaults, keeps bank balance sheets honest.
- **`production`** — BOM-driven production: inputs, energy and labour constrain real output.
- **`household_consumption`** — Hourly household purchases at the world's physical consumption rates.
- **`cohort_consumption`** — Aggregate consumption, welfare, sentiment and unrest for the cohort population.
- **`facts`** — Event → objective Fact, with deterministic rendering and no LLM.
- **`media`** — Outlet pickup, framing, narratives and publication reach.
- **`propagation`** — Diffusion of facts to individuals and cohorts, with decay and distortion.
- **`history`** — Indexes significant events into the in-world chronicle and topic counters.
- **`demographics`** — Monthly ageing, mortality, fertility and migration for individuals and cohorts.
- **`research`** — Funded research projects, discoveries, adoption and frontier expansion.
- **`culture`** — Emergent slang, memes, movements and conspiracies driven by lived conditions.
